# Class 11–12 Maths App: Chapters, Concepts, 108 Games and Simulations

*Content map for the app: 27 chapters, each with concepts, a mind map, a flowchart, 4 games and 1 simulation. Check chapter names against the current NCERT/CBSE syllabus before release, as chapters are sometimes rationalised.*

## How the content is organised

Every chapter has the same five parts, so one template builds all 27 chapters:

- **Concepts:** the key definitions, formulas and results, shown as short cards.
- **Mind map:** one central node with 5–6 branches, as in the Chapter screen.
- **Flowchart:** a step-by-step path for solving the chapter's typical problem.
- **Games:** 4 per chapter (27 × 4 = **108 games**).
- **Simulation:** 1 interactive visual per chapter (27 simulations).

Game mechanics to reuse: Quiz Blitz (timed MCQs), Formula Match (pair cards), Step Builder (arrange solution steps), Graph Spotter (identify a curve), Fill the Map (complete a mind map), Error Hunter (find the wrong step), Speed Calc (answer before the timer ends), Puzzle Unlock (solve to open the next level).

# Class 11

## 1. Sets

**Concepts:** types of sets, subsets, power set, Venn diagrams, union, intersection, difference, complement, De Morgan's laws, counting formula for two and three sets. **Mind map:** representation, types, subsets, operations, laws, Venn applications. **Flowchart:** read the word problem, define sets, draw Venn diagram, apply n(A∪B) formula, solve. **Games:** Set Sorter (drag elements into sets); Venn Painter (shade the region asked); De Morgan Duel; Power Set Counter. **Simulation:** drag circles to see union, intersection and complement update live.

## 2. Relations and Functions

**Concepts:** ordered pairs, Cartesian product, relations, domain, codomain, range, functions, real functions and their graphs. **Mind map:** Cartesian product, relation, function, domain and range, special functions, graphs. **Flowchart:** given a rule, is it a function? Then find domain, then range, then sketch. **Games:** Function or Not; Domain Detective; Range Hunter; Graph Spotter (modulus, greatest integer, signum). **Simulation:** arrow diagram that connects inputs to outputs and flags violations of the function rule.

## 3. Trigonometric Functions

**Concepts:** radian and degree measure, unit circle, trigonometric ratios of any angle, sum and difference formulas, double and triple angle, product-sum formulas, general solutions of equations. **Mind map:** angles, unit circle, ratios, identities, equations, graphs. **Flowchart:** identify the equation form, reduce to one ratio, find the principal value, write the general solution. **Games:** Unit Circle Race; Identity Match; Equation Puzzle; Graph Spotter (sin, cos, tan shifts). **Simulation:** a point moving on the unit circle draws the sine and cosine waves, with sliders for amplitude and phase.

## 4. Complex Numbers and Quadratic Equations

**Concepts:** imaginary unit, powers of i, algebra of complex numbers, modulus, conjugate, Argand plane, polar form, quadratic equations with complex roots. **Mind map:** i and its powers, algebra, conjugate and modulus, Argand plane, polar form, quadratics. **Flowchart:** compute the discriminant, if negative write roots as a ± bi, plot on the Argand plane. **Games:** Power of i Spinner; Conjugate Match; Argand Plotter; Root Finder. **Simulation:** drag a point on the Argand plane to see modulus, argument and conjugate update, and watch multiplication rotate points.

## 5. Linear Inequalities

**Concepts:** inequalities in one and two variables, number line solutions, graphical solution of systems. **Mind map:** rules, one variable, two variables, number line, shaded regions, systems. **Flowchart:** solve for the variable, flip the sign when multiplying by a negative, mark the number line. **Games:** Sign Flip Trap; Number Line Jump; Shade the Region; Inequality Match. **Simulation:** draw a line and shade the half-plane, then add a second inequality to see the feasible region.

## 6. Permutations and Combinations

**Concepts:** fundamental principle of counting, factorial, nPr, nCr, arrangements with repetition, circular arrangements basics. **Mind map:** counting principle, factorial, permutation, combination, restrictions, applications. **Flowchart:** does order matter? If yes use nPr, if no use nCr, then apply restrictions. **Games:** Order or Not; Arrange the Word; Team Picker; Counting Escape Room. **Simulation:** arrange coloured tokens in slots and watch the count of arrangements grow.

## 7. Binomial Theorem

**Concepts:** binomial expansion for positive integers, Pascal's triangle, general term, middle term, coefficients. **Mind map:** expansion, Pascal's triangle, general term, middle term, properties, applications. **Flowchart:** write the general term, set the power of x equal to the one asked, solve for r. **Games:** Pascal Path; General Term Finder; Middle Term Hunt; Coefficient Match. **Simulation:** an interactive Pascal's triangle that highlights which row expands which power.

## 8. Sequences and Series

**Concepts:** arithmetic and geometric progressions, nth term, sum formulas, AM-GM inequality, special series. **Mind map:** AP, GP, means, AM-GM, special sums, applications. **Flowchart:** identify AP or GP from the ratio or difference, pick the formula, solve for the unknown. **Games:** AP or GP; Next Term Sprint; Sum Race; Mean Match. **Simulation:** adjust first term and ratio to watch a GP grow, converge or oscillate.

## 9. Straight Lines

**Concepts:** slope, forms of line equations, distance of a point from a line, angle between lines, parallel and perpendicular conditions. **Mind map:** slope, equation forms, intercepts, distance, angle, conditions. **Flowchart:** choose the form for the given data, write the equation, simplify to standard form. **Games:** Slope Surfer; Equation Form Match; Distance Dash; Parallel or Perpendicular. **Simulation:** drag two points to see slope, intercepts and the equation in every form.

## 10. Conic Sections

**Concepts:** circle, parabola, ellipse, hyperbola, standard equations, focus, directrix, eccentricity, latus rectum. **Mind map:** cone slicing, circle, parabola, ellipse, hyperbola, eccentricity. **Flowchart:** compare the equation with standard forms, read a, b, c, then find foci and eccentricity. **Games:** Name That Conic; Focus Finder; Eccentricity Sort; Conic Builder. **Simulation:** slice a cone with a plane and watch the conic change.

## 11. Introduction to Three Dimensional Geometry

**Concepts:** coordinate axes and planes, octants, distance formula, section formula. **Mind map:** axes, planes, octants, distance, section formula, midpoint. **Flowchart:** write both points, apply the distance or section formula, simplify. **Games:** Octant Finder; 3D Distance Dash; Section Splitter; Plot the Point. **Simulation:** a rotatable 3D axes view where points can be placed and measured.

## 12. Limits and Derivatives

**Concepts:** limits, standard limits, algebra of limits, derivative from first principle, rules of differentiation. **Mind map:** limit idea, standard limits, derivative, first principle, rules, meaning. **Flowchart:** substitute, if indeterminate factor or rationalise, then apply a standard limit. **Games:** Limit Lander; Rule Match (product, quotient, chain); Slope Zoom; Derivative Duel. **Simulation:** zoom a secant line into a tangent and watch the slope approach the derivative.

## 13. Statistics

**Concepts:** mean deviation, variance, standard deviation for ungrouped and grouped data. **Mind map:** spread, mean deviation, variance, standard deviation, grouped data, comparison. **Flowchart:** find the mean, find deviations, square or take absolute values, divide, compare. **Games:** Spread Sorter; Deviation Dash; Mean or Median; Data Detective. **Simulation:** drag data points on a number line and watch mean and standard deviation change.

## 14. Probability

**Concepts:** sample space, events, algebra of events, axiomatic probability, addition rule. **Mind map:** experiment, sample space, events, rules, mutually exclusive, applications. **Flowchart:** list the sample space, count favourable outcomes, divide, check the rules. **Games:** Sample Space Builder; Dice Duel; Card Draw; Event Match. **Simulation:** roll dice or flip coins many times and watch relative frequency approach probability.

# Class 12

## 15. Relations and Functions

**Concepts:** types of relations, equivalence relations, one-one, onto, bijective functions, composition, invertible functions. **Mind map:** relations, equivalence, injective, surjective, composition, inverse. **Flowchart:** test reflexive, then symmetric, then transitive; for functions test one-one, then onto. **Games:** Equivalence Checker; One-One or Onto; Compose It; Inverse Finder. **Simulation:** arrow diagrams that show injective and surjective behaviour live.

## 16. Inverse Trigonometric Functions

**Concepts:** domain and range of each inverse function, principal values, graphs, properties. **Mind map:** sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹, tan⁻¹, others, principal values, identities. **Flowchart:** identify the function, check the domain, find the principal value in its range. **Games:** Principal Value Race; Domain Range Match; Identity Simplifier; Graph Mirror. **Simulation:** reflect a trigonometric graph across y = x to produce its inverse.

## 17. Matrices

**Concepts:** types of matrices, operations, transpose, symmetric and skew-symmetric, invertible matrices. **Mind map:** types, operations, transpose, special matrices, inverse, applications. **Flowchart:** check orders are compatible, perform the operation, simplify. **Games:** Order Checker; Matrix Multiply Maze; Symmetric or Not; Transpose Flip. **Simulation:** edit a 2×2 matrix to see how it transforms a shape.

## 18. Determinants

**Concepts:** determinant value, minors, cofactors, adjoint, inverse, area of triangle, solving linear systems. **Mind map:** value, properties, minors and cofactors, adjoint, inverse, systems. **Flowchart:** compute the determinant, if zero the system is singular, else find the adjoint and inverse. **Games:** Expand It; Property Spotter; Cofactor Grid; System Solver. **Simulation:** see the area of the parallelogram change as matrix entries change.

## 19. Continuity and Differentiability

**Concepts:** continuity at a point, differentiability, chain rule, implicit and logarithmic differentiation, parametric forms, second derivative, Rolle's and mean value theorems. **Mind map:** continuity, differentiability, chain rule, implicit, parametric, theorems. **Flowchart:** check left limit, right limit and value; then check the derivative on both sides. **Games:** Continuity Checker; Chain Rule Chain; Implicit Maze; Theorem Match. **Simulation:** a function with a movable break showing where continuity and differentiability fail.

## 20. Application of Derivatives

**Concepts:** rate of change, increasing and decreasing functions, tangents and normals, approximations, maxima and minima. **Mind map:** rates, monotonicity, tangent and normal, approximation, maxima and minima. **Flowchart:** find f′(x), set it to zero, test with f″ or sign change, interpret. **Games:** Rate Racer; Tangent Builder; Max or Min; Optimise the Box. **Simulation:** slide a point along a curve to see tangent, slope sign and turning points.

## 21. Integrals

**Concepts:** indefinite integrals, substitution, by parts, partial fractions, standard forms, definite integrals and properties. **Mind map:** antiderivative, substitution, by parts, partial fractions, definite integrals, properties. **Flowchart:** try a standard form, then substitution, then by parts (ILATE), then partial fractions. **Games:** Method Matcher; ILATE Order; Integral Quiz Blitz; Area Under the Curve. **Simulation:** Riemann rectangles that refine toward the exact integral.

## 22. Application of Integrals

**Concepts:** area under a curve, area between curves, simple regions. **Mind map:** area under curve, between curves, limits, symmetry, regions. **Flowchart:** sketch, find intersection points, set limits, integrate the top minus the bottom. **Games:** Shade the Area; Limit Finder; Area Estimator; Curve Cutter. **Simulation:** drag the limits to see the shaded area update.

## 23. Differential Equations

**Concepts:** order and degree, general and particular solutions, variable separable, homogeneous, linear first order. **Mind map:** order and degree, formation, separable, homogeneous, linear, applications. **Flowchart:** classify the equation, pick the method, integrate, apply the initial condition. **Games:** Order and Degree; Method Picker; Separate the Variables; Solution Check. **Simulation:** a slope field where clicking a start point traces the solution curve.

## 24. Vector Algebra

**Concepts:** types of vectors, addition, scalar multiplication, direction cosines, dot product, cross product, projection. **Mind map:** types, operations, components, dot product, cross product, applications. **Flowchart:** write components, choose dot for angle or projection, choose cross for area or perpendicular. **Games:** Vector Add Race; Dot or Cross; Direction Cosine Quiz; Area Maker. **Simulation:** drag two vectors to see sum, dot product, cross product and the angle.

## 25. Three Dimensional Geometry

**Concepts:** direction cosines and ratios, equations of a line, angle between lines, shortest distance, plane equations. **Mind map:** direction ratios, line, plane, angles, distance, intersection. **Flowchart:** write the line or plane in vector form, convert to Cartesian, apply the distance or angle formula. **Games:** Direction Ratio Match; Line Builder; Skew or Parallel; Plane Finder. **Simulation:** a rotatable 3D scene showing lines, planes and their shortest distance.

## 26. Linear Programming

**Concepts:** objective function, constraints, feasible region, corner point method. **Mind map:** objective, constraints, feasible region, corner points, optimal value. **Flowchart:** write constraints, draw the region, find corner points, evaluate the objective, pick best. **Games:** Constraint Builder; Feasible Region Shader; Corner Point Hunt; Profit Maximiser. **Simulation:** drag constraint lines and see the feasible region and best corner update.

## 27. Probability

**Concepts:** conditional probability, multiplication rule, independent events, Bayes' theorem, random variables, binomial distribution. **Mind map:** conditional, independence, total probability, Bayes, distributions, mean and variance. **Flowchart:** build a tree, multiply along branches, add across branches, apply Bayes if reversed. **Games:** Tree Builder; Independent or Not; Bayes Detective; Binomial Bet. **Simulation:** a probability tree with sliders that update posterior probabilities live.
